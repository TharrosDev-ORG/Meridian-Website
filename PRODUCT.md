# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one room:

1. **Ottawa students** (Carleton, uOttawa, Algonquin) deciding whether this society is worth their evening. They arrive from Instagram or word of mouth, mostly on phones. The job: understand what Meridian is in ten seconds, register in under a minute.
2. **Potential "clients"**: professionals, founders, scholars, and alumni being invited to speak (or considering sponsoring). They arrive from an email or LinkedIn link, mostly on desktop. The job: judge in one scroll whether this organization is serious enough to lend their name to.

The second audience is why restraint matters. The site must impress without performing.

## Product Purpose

The website is The Meridian Society's core identity: an independent, student-run speaker forum and social community in Ottawa. It exists to (a) convert students into registered members, (b) convince accomplished professionals to apply to speak, and (c) signal institutional seriousness despite having no institution behind it. Success: registrations grow, speakers say yes, and nobody mistakes it for a campus club template.

## Brand Personality

Composed, ambitious, candle-lit. The feel of a lecture hall after hours: dark wood, a single warm light, a serious conversation about to start. Premium without luxury-brand coldness; youthful ambition expressed through discipline, not noise.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing slop: gradient meshes, glassmorphism cards, hero-metric grids.
- Generic student-club sites: bright primaries, stock campus photos, Linktree energy.
- Crypto/agency maximalism: scroll-jacking, pinned takeover sections, WebGL for its own sake.

## Design Principles

1. **Enhance, never gate.** Every word is readable with JavaScript off. Motion is layered on top of an already-complete page.
2. **Restraint is the luxury.** One idea per fold. The dark sections earn drama through scale and stillness, not effect count.
3. **The room, not the poster.** Dark ink sections evoke the forum itself; cream sections are the daylight reading layer. Alternation is the narrative.
4. **Mobile gets the fast path.** Heavy motion (Lenis, Three.js, scrub) is a desktop privilege; phones get the instant, legible version.
5. **Type carries the brand.** Cormorant Garamond at oversized scale against Barlow Condensed metadata is the identity; everything else supports that contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast minimums on both cream and dark surfaces (body text ≥ 4.5:1).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` gets full parity: static hero, instant reveals, native scroll.
- Skip link, visible gold focus rings (legible on dark), full keyboard navigation of menu, tabs, and accordion.
- Touch devices skip hover-dependent affordances; horizontal scrollers keep swipe hints.
