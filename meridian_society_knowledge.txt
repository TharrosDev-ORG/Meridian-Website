SECTION 1 — IDENTITY & OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Full name: The Meridian Society
Type: Independent, student-run organization
Founded: 2025
Location: Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
Tagline: "A Place for Discourse"
Guiding philosophy: "Shaped by the effort you put in, your input defines your outcome by building knowledge, connections, and opportunity."
Volume: Vol. I · 2025–2026 · Ottawa

The Meridian Society is entirely student-built, student-owned, and student-operated. It has no institutional affiliation, no political alignment, and no corporate backing. It is not a university club, not a faculty program, and not a government-affiliated initiative.

The Society was founded to connect motivated students with professionals, alumni, and scholars who can broaden their perspective, while simultaneously building a strong community of students and young professionals in Ottawa.

Taglines and brand phrases used publicly:
- "A Place for Discourse"
- "A room you'll want to be a part of."
- "Not a major, not a club, but a sensibility."
- "Intentionally Not for Everyone."
- "Two programs, one Society."
- "Speaker forums that broaden perspective and social gatherings that build community."
- "An Independent Student Organization · Ottawa · Est. 2025"

SECTION 2 — MISSION & PHILOSOPHY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Core purpose: To create a space for discourse and the exchange of lived experience. Through events, discussions, and gatherings, the Society aims to help members better navigate an increasingly complex and competitive world.

The Meridian Society is designed for motivated individuals who take ideas seriously. It is not trying to appeal to everyone — the focus is on quality of engagement over quantity of attendance.

The Society gathers three broad groups:
  01. Students & Alumni — Motivated individuals from Carleton University, uOttawa, and Algonquin College, and anyone drawn to the work the Society does.
  02. Speakers & Professionals — People across law, business, policy, media, and beyond, with lived experience.
  03. Scholars & Thinkers — Academics and researchers whose work challenges assumptions, opens new territory, and gives students something to think about.

What the Society is NOT:
- Not a resume workshop or career placement office
- Not a political advocacy or activism group
- Not a startup incubator or pitch competition
- Not a closed, elite, invitation-only circle
- Not a career fair or networking event in the traditional sense

Direct quote from the website on this: "We are not a resume workshop, a pitch competition, or a networking event. We have no political agenda or exclusive membership criteria, and we are fully student-run and independent. What we offer is simple: a room with the right people and the willingness to listen."

SECTION 4 — PROGRAMS (EVENTS)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Meridian Society runs two core programs:

───────────────────────────────────────
PROGRAM I — THE SPEAKER FORUM (Flagship / Signature Program)
───────────────────────────────────────

Description: The flagship event — a platform for professionals and academics to share lived experience with students. Not lectures — open dialogues with the practical insight, ideas, and networks not found in the classroom.

Format specifications:
  - Length: 30–45 minute presentation
  - Format: Followed by a Q&A
  - Audience size: 20–30 students
  - Who attends: Society Members
  - Location: Ottawa, Canada
  - Subject fields: Policy, academia, entrepreneurship, law, business, politics, and beyond

Three core pillars of the Speaker Forum:
  01. Dialogue Over Noise — Small, curated audiences ensure every student has the chance to ask questions and engage directly with the speaker.
  02. Vetted Insight — Speakers share honest perspectives, practical advice, and knowledge drawn from experience.
  03. Beyond the Classroom — Bridge the gap between theory and practice by exploring how ideas are applied in real-world contexts.

Key distinction: These are not traditional lectures. They are open dialogues designed to surface practical knowledge and real-world perspective that students wouldn't encounter in a standard academic setting.

Inaugural event: Fall 2026

───────────────────────────────────────
PROGRAM II — SOCIAL GATHERINGS
───────────────────────────────────────

Description: Beyond the speaker series. Socials keep it simple — relaxed settings, good energy, and the kind of room where connections happen naturally. Driven people, real conversations, fresh perspectives, no pressure.

Four types of social gatherings:

  1. THEMED GATHERINGS — Bar Nights & Lounges
     High-impact social environments where students socialize and expand their circles.

  2. SMALL FORMAT — Coffee & Conversation
     Low-pressure meetups designed for smaller groups. Perfect for deeper dialogue and getting to know the community.

  3. IMPACT DRIVEN — Fundraisers & Galas
     Specialized events dedicated to raising support for society missions while bringing the community together for a cause.

  4. ACADEMIC PLUS — Social Mixers
     Post-Speaker Forum gatherings where students and guest speakers continue the conversation in a casual setting.

NOTE: The Society does not operate a live events calendar on the website. All real-time event announcements are made on Instagram (@Meridian.Society). There is no dynamic calendar — event information is delivered via member email invitations and Instagram.

SECTION 5 — MEMBERSHIP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cost: Completely free. No registration fees, dues, or hidden costs. Membership is free.

Eligibility: Open to anyone with an interest in the ideas the Society explores. There is no geographic restriction and no academic affiliation required. No specific program or institution is required. Motivated, curious students and young professionals in the Ottawa area are the primary audience.

Target audience: Students and young professionals from Carleton University, uOttawa, Algonquin College, and the broader Ottawa community.

Commitment: None required. Programming is modular — members attend what suits their interests. There is no mandatory attendance. Membership is a persistent credential meant to be used as it suits you.

What happens after you register:
  - You receive priority announcements and direct invitations to all upcoming Meridian events.
  - Zero-spam policy is maintained.
  - You can also follow @Meridian.Society on Instagram for real-time updates.
  - You receive your digital Member ID card immediately after registration.

Five membership benefits:
  01. Speaker Events — Be the first to know. Members receive all announcements, event schedules, and invitations to Meridian speaker events. Priority access means you're in the room when it matters.
  02. Social Gatherings — Members are invited to Meridian social events: bar nights, casual meetups, and community gatherings that happen between the formal programming.
  03. Professional Exposure — The people speaking at Meridian are professionals, alumni, and scholars from fields you may never have crossed otherwise. Membership puts their stories and perspectives directly in front of you.
  04. A Real Community — Meridian builds a circle over time. Members connect with people who share genuine curiosity. Not a major, not a club, but a sensibility.
  05. Your Peers — The people in the room are half the reason to show up. Meridian members are Ottawa students who take ideas seriously.

Registration collects: your name, contact email address, current or most recent institution (if applicable), and interests from a short checklist. No payment information, phone number, or government-issued identifier is collected.

SECTION 7 — APPLYING TO SPEAK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Who should apply: Professionals, founders, scholars, alumni, and specialists with a story or idea worth sharing. The target is anyone with lived experience who wants direct access to an engaged, vetted student audience in a room built for real conversation.

How to apply: Via the official speaker application form at /apply on the website. Speakers may also be nominated via email (meridiansocietycanada@gmail.com) using the subject line "Speaker Nomination."

Review process: The society team reviews every proposal to ensure a rigorous and diverse dialogue for members. The Society prioritizes topics that challenge conventional wisdom. Review window is 7–10 days after submission.

Speaker form collects:
  - Full name and email
  - Current role/title and organization
  - Primary classification (Academic / Industry / Entrepreneur / Policy / NGO / Other)
  - Areas of expertise (International Relations, Global Health, Economics, Tech/AI, Climate & Environment, Human Rights, Business & Finance, Education, Law, Other)
  - Proposed presentation title
  - Topic overview
  - Key takeaways (3–5 main points)
  - Professional bio
  - Preferred format (Keynote Presentation, Panel Discussion, Interactive Workshop, Informal Fireside Chat, Open Forum / Q&A)
  - Availability window (Fall 2026, Winter 2027, Spring/Summer 2027, Flexible/Rolling, or specific date/window)
  - Location constraints
  - Past speaking experience (yes/no)
  - Portfolio or past talk link (optional)
  - LinkedIn URL (optional)
  - Other social media (optional)
  - How they heard about the Society
  - Additional notes (optional)

What speakers get: Direct access to a curated, engaged audience of 20–30 motivated Ottawa students. Events are held in Ottawa. The format is a 30–45 minute presentation followed by an open Q&A.

SECTION 9 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Is membership free?
A: Yes. Membership is completely free. There are no registration fees, dues, or hidden costs to join The Meridian Society.

Q: Who can join?
A: Any motivated, curious student in the Ottawa area is welcome to register. You don't need to be affiliated with a specific academic program or institution to join the conversation.

Q: What happens after I register?
A: You will receive priority announcements and direct invitations to all upcoming Meridian events. The Society maintains a zero-spam policy, and membership involves no mandatory commitments. You can also follow @Meridian.Society on Instagram for real-time updates.

Q: Do I have to attend every event?
A: No. Programming is modular. You are welcome to attend only the events that align with your interests. Membership is a persistent credential meant to be used as it suits you.

Q: What is my Member ID, and why is it important?
A: Your Member ID is your unique identifier within The Meridian Society's registry. It grants you access to private events and is your record of membership. You can download your digital ID card immediately after registration and present it at venue check-in.

Q: Is the Meridian Society affiliated with a university or college?
A: No. The Society is fully independent — not a university club, not a faculty program, and not affiliated with any institution, government body, or political organization.

Q: I'm not from Carleton or uOttawa — can I still join?
A: Yes. Membership is open to anyone. There is no geographic restriction and no academic affiliation required. The Society welcomes students from Carleton, uOttawa, Algonquin College, and the broader Ottawa community — as well as young professionals who are not currently enrolled.

Q: How do I find out about upcoming events?
A: Two ways: (1) Register as a member to receive direct email invitations. (2) Follow @Meridian.Society on Instagram for real-time announcements.

Q: Is there a calendar of events on the website?
A: No. The website does not have a live event calendar. All real-time event information is shared through member email invitations and on Instagram (@Meridian.Society).

Q: How many students attend each Speaker Forum?
A: Audiences are intentionally kept small — 20 to 30 students — to ensure meaningful, direct engagement with the speaker.

Q: What subjects do speakers cover?
A: The range includes policy, academia, entrepreneurship, law, business, politics, international relations, global health, economics, technology and AI, climate and environment, human rights, and more.

Q: I want to speak at a Meridian event. How do I apply?
A: Visit /apply on the website to submit a speaker application. Alternatively, send a speaker nomination email to meridiansocietycanada@gmail.com with the subject "Speaker Nomination." The review window is 7–10 days.

Q: What formats are available for speakers?
A: Keynote Presentation, Panel Discussion, Interactive Workshop, Informal Fireside Chat, or Open Forum / Q&A.

Q: When is the inaugural event?
A: The inaugural event is scheduled for Fall 2026.

Q: Does the Society have a political agenda?
A: No. The Meridian Society has no political agenda. It is not a political advocacy or activism group.

Q: Is there a minimum commitment to stay a member?
A: None. Membership is free, voluntary, and involves no mandatory attendance or ongoing obligation.

Q: How do I get my Member ID card?
A: Your digital Member ID card is generated automatically upon completing registration. You can download it immediately from the registration success page.

Q: What does the Member ID card look like?
A: It is a 1200×1200 px digital PNG card. It features your full name, unique member number (format: M{YY}-{NNNN}, e.g. M26-1001), and the date you joined. The design uses the Society's cream-and-ink colour palette with gold accents.

Q: Can my member number change?
A: No. Member numbers are permanent and immutable once assigned.

Q: How do I contact the Society?
A: By email at meridiansocietycanada@gmail.com or on Instagram at @Meridian.Society. For speaker applications, use the form at /apply.

Q: How do I request deletion of my personal information?
A: Email meridiansocietycanada@gmail.com from the address you registered with. Deletion is confirmed within five business days.

Q: What personal information does the Society collect?
A: Name, contact email, current or most recent institution (optional), and selected interests. No payment information, phone number, or government ID is ever collected.

Q: Does the Society use advertising cookies or tracking pixels?
A: No. The site does not use advertising cookies, Google Analytics, or third-party tracking pixels.

Q: Who do I contact for press or partnership inquiries?
A: Email meridiansocietycanada@gmail.com. All media and partnership inquiries are routed to the appropriate organizers within 48 hours.

Q: Where is the Society based?
A: Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.

Q: Who founded the Society?
A: Magnus Abdelnour, a student at Carleton University studying Global and International Studies (European and Russian Studies). He founded the Society to bring journalists, founders, scholars, and accomplished professionals to share knowledge with students.

Q: Who is on the team?
A: Magnus Abdelnour (Founder & President) and Colin Sherwood (Event Coordinator). The team is actively growing.

Q: What is the Society's Instagram handle?
A: @Meridian.Society
