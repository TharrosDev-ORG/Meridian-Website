THE MERIDIAN SOCIETY - KNOWLEDGE BASE

GENERAL OVERVIEW
The Meridian Society is an independent, student-run organization based in Ottawa, Canada, established in 2025. It serves as a foundational platform connecting motivated students with accomplished professionals, alumni, and scholars to broaden perspectives and build a strong community. The society is entirely student-built, owned, and operated, without any institutional or political affiliations.

MISSION & PHILOSOPHY
- Purpose: To create a space for discourse and the exchange of lived experience.
- Philosophy: "Shaped by the effort you put in, your input defines your outcome by building knowledge, connections, and opportunity."
- Independence: Fully autonomous; not a university club, resume workshop, or political advocacy group.
- Audience: Motivated individuals from Carleton University, uOttawa, Algonquin College, and the broader Ottawa student/young professional community.

CORE PROGRAMS

1. THE SPEAKER FORUM (Signature Program)
   - Description: A flagship series of open dialogues where professionals and academics share practical insights not found in the classroom.
   - Format: 30–45 minute presentations followed by an open Q&A.
   - Setting: Curated, small audiences (20–30 students) to ensure meaningful engagement.
   - Subject Areas: Policy, Law, Business, Media, Academia, Entrepreneurship, and beyond.

2. SOCIAL GATHERINGS
   - Description: Events designed to build community in relaxed, high-energy settings.
   - Formats:
     - Themed Gatherings: Bar nights and lounges for social expansion.
     - Small Format: "Coffee & Conversation" meetups for deeper dialogue.
     - Impact Driven: Fundraisers and galas supporting society missions.
     - Academic Plus: Social mixers following Speaker Forums.

MEMBERSHIP
- Cost: Completely free. No registration fees or hidden dues.
- Eligibility: Open to any curious and motivated student or young professional in the Ottawa area.
- Commitment: None required. Programming is modular; members attend what suits their interests.
- Benefits:
  - Priority access to all Speaker Forum events and social announcements.
  - Direct invitations to community mixers and gatherings.
  - Connection to a vetted network of peers and professionals.
  - Digital Member ID card for rapid venue check-in.

MEMBER ID CARD
- Purpose: A unique identifier within the Society’s registry.
- Usage: Grants access to private events and serves as a record of membership.
- Generation: A digital card (1200x1200px) is generated upon registration, featuring the member's name, unique number, and joining date.

SPEAKER OPPORTUNITIES (APPLY TO SPEAK)
- Target: Professionals, founders, scholars, and specialists with a story or idea worth sharing.
- Offering: Direct access to an engaged, vetted student audience in a room built for real conversation.
- Application: Interested speakers can apply via the official website portal or be nominated via email.

WHAT THE MERIDIAN SOCIETY IS NOT
- Not a career placement office or resume workshop.
- Not a political advocacy or activism group.
- Not a startup incubator or pitch competition.
- Not a closed, elite, invitation-only circle.

CONTACT & CONNECT
- Location: Ottawa, Canada.
- Digital Presence: Official updates and real-time announcements are primarily shared via Instagram (@Meridian.Society).
- Contact: General inquiries and speaker nominations are handled via email.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
- Is membership free? Yes, 100%.
- Who can join? Any curious student in Ottawa; no specific program affiliation required.
- After registration? You get priority invites. Zero-spam policy.
- Attend every event? No, attend what you like.
- Member ID importance? It's your ticket to private events and rapid check-in.
